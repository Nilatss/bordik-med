- DAU/MAU stickiness ≥20%
- Retention D7 ≥45% (врач), D30 ≥28%
- Calculator-to-course conversion ≥5%
- Time-to-result медиана ≤3 сек, ≤2 тапа
- NPS ≥40
- CME-completion rate ≥40%
- AI-usage ≥30% / token cost ≤$0.30/MAU
